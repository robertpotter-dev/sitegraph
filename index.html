The Gap

A middle ground between fragile spreadsheets and heavy enterprise bloat.

🔗

Structured like a platform.

Validated IPAM, typed interfaces, and graph invariant checks prevent impossible states like duplicate IPs or cyclic racks.

🔗

Strict Graph Enforcement

Spreadsheets flatten complex networks into fragile, disconnected rows. By natively modeling infrastructure as a property graph, SiteGraph ensures every asset is relationally aware—guaranteeing an accurate, interconnected topology that flat files simply cannot represent.

📦

Single-file application.

Requires no installation or backend deployment. The application is delivered as a single, self-contained HTML file weighing less than 1MB.

📦

Client-Side Compute

By leveraging modern Web APIs and IndexedDB, expensive graph traversals and schema validations execute entirely on your device's CPU. There is no backend latency, no database scaling, and no Docker container to maintain.

🔐

Sovereign by design.

Your topology data is a single JSON document. Encrypt it, version-control it, or air-gap it on your own terms.

🔐

Direct Data Custody

Maintain direct custody of your network data with zero SiteGraph telemetry or SaaS exhaust. Whether stored strictly locally or hosted on your own private infrastructure, your topology remains within systems you choose, integrating seamlessly into your existing enterprise compliance, auditing, and backup workflows.

Automation

A reliable foundation for automation and AI.

Your network data belongs to you. By structuring your topology as a strictly local property graph, SiteGraph unlocks multiple export options that natively feed your AI and automation pipelines. SiteGraph processes topology data locally and does not transmit it to a third-party cloud; you control any files you export or share.

  • Neo4j Cypher Direct export to graph databases.
  • Spreadsheets Standardized tabular CSV formats.
  • LLM Digest Optimized for AI context windows.
  • Canonical JSON The schema-validated source of truth.
In Practice

Designed for the realities of physical infrastructure.

For Field Technicians

Offline-first Service Worker architecture ensures access deep in the server closet without an internet connection. Real schema enforcement turns Devices, IPs, and People into strictly typed entities, not just text in a spreadsheet cell.

For Network Engineers

SiteGraph guarantees field documentation is captured with strict validation and precision. The single-file architecture delegates security to existing IT policies, ensuring sensitive topologies never have to touch a third-party cloud.

For Management

A portable, diffable documentation asset that stays securely on-premises to satisfy compliance audits. Zero infrastructure tax and clear timestamped logs streamline operations and lower the total cost of ownership.
